How prices were checked

Each deal was found and checked in person on Nov 27, 2026: the sale price and the usual price were read on the store’s own product page, or on a deal roundup that names the store. Every card lists its check time and its source. Discount % = (usual − sale) ÷ usual.

“Usual” is the store’s list price or comparable value. Where that number is a store’s comparable value rather than a recent selling price, the card says so — because a big % off a number nobody paid is not a big saving.

  • New low lowest price we have seen for this product
  • Strong price genuinely good, at or near the low
  • Routine sale a normal, modest cut
  • Inflated the list price is set high, so the % overstates the saving

“Best deals first” ranks by discount % weighted by signal, so a 67% cut on a real low outranks an 89% cut on an inflated list price. Prices change fast on Black Friday — re-check the store page before you buy. All prices in USD.
